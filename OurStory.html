Two Floors
Authentic Italian Bar, Ristorante, Wine Bar, School & Artisanal Labs

Gran Caffè L'Aquila was a landmark café located on Piazza Duomo in the capital city of the Abruzzo region. In 2007 Gran Caffè L'Aquila won Café of the Year for all of Italy, before being tragically damaged in the devastating earthquake of 2009 that struck the city.

In 2012, Italian gelato champion Stefano Biasini met award-winning local restaurateur Riccardo Longo, sparking a partnership that led to the rebirth of one of Italy's most beloved cafés in Philadelphia.

The entire restaurant was designed and built in Italy and shipped across the Atlantic for installation. After three years of meticulous design and build-out, the most authentic Italian café in America opened on December 24th, 2014. The first floor welcomes guests into a traditional Italian bar, while the second floor houses our ristorante, wine bar, cultural and language school, and our coffee roasting and gelato laboratories — complemented by our online market offering products of excellence from Italy.
